Cue — Privacy & Data Deletion

Cue is a personal TV and movie tracker that talks directly to your own Trakt account.

No server, nothing stored off your device

Cue runs no server of its own — there is no backend, no Cue account, and no database Cue controls. Everything Cue keeps lives only on the device you use it on: your Trakt sign-in token, a local cache of the shows and history Cue reads from Trakt, and your in-app settings. All of your sync state (watch history, watchlist, ratings, progress) lives in your own Trakt account, reached directly over HTTPS.

No analytics, no telemetry

Cue collects no analytics and sends no telemetry. It makes no network requests except directly to Trakt (and the image hosts Trakt points at) to do the tracking you asked for.

Deleting your data

  • Cue's on-device data: open Settings → Disconnect Trakt. Disconnecting revokes this device's access to your Trakt account, signs you out, and deletes the local cache and Trakt token stored on the device. Uninstalling the app removes the same data.
  • Your Trakt account itself: Cue cannot delete it — Cue has no account of its own and no server-side copy of your data. Only Trakt can delete a Trakt account, at app.trakt.tv/settings/advanced.
